## Plastome Summary

The selected *Ocimum basilicum* chloroplast genome is 152,407 base pairs long and has a circular topology. The Galaxy FASTA Statistics analysis identified one sequence, with a GC content of 37.84%. The sequence contains no gaps and no ambiguous N bases.

The annotated genome contains protein-coding genes, transfer RNA genes, and ribosomal RNA genes. Features associated with photosynthesis, transcription, translation, ATP synthesis, and other plastid functions are present.

## Gene Content

Based on the annotation of NC_035143.1:

- Total annotated gene features: 129
- Protein-coding genes: 84
- tRNA genes: 37
- rRNA genes: 8
- Annotated pseudogenes: 0

Representative plastid genes identified include genes from the photosynthesis-related groups (*psa* and *psb*), ATP synthase (*atp*), cytochrome complex (*pet*), carbon fixation (*rbcL*), RNA polymerase (*rpo*), and ribosomal protein (*rpl* and *rps*) groups.

Other notable genes include *matK*, *clpP*, *accD*, *cemA*, and several *ycf* genes.

## Important Observations

The genome has the typical compact organization associated with plant chloroplast genomes, including duplicated features associated with inverted-repeat regions.

Several genes occur in duplicated copies, including *ycf2* and *rpl23*, as well as several tRNA genes.

Genes with intron-containing annotations are also present, including examples such as *rpoC1*, *atpF*, *rpl2*, *ndhA*, and *rps16*.

No annotated pseudogenes were identified in the NC_035143.1 record.
